# 拾页 / The Private Archive

## Direction

A precise, quiet editorial archive: charcoal navigation, warm porcelain workspace, generous typography, larger visual cards with square cover framing, and a restrained copper accent. Real collected media is the visual focus. Avoid oversized marketing heroes, repeated metrics, dense tiny text, decorative dashboards, and attention-grabbing animation.

The ui-ux-pro-max Minimalism & Swiss Style match is relevant to a professional tool; its grid, contrast and restrained transitions are adopted. Marketing landing-page patterns returned by the generator are not used. The detailed layout follows the user's library workflow and split media/text detail reference.

## Tokens

- Primitive: ink `#272928`, charcoal `#222524`, porcelain `#f6f5f1`, white `#ffffff`, copper `#9f6448`, stone `#e5e2da`.
- Semantic: primary action = ink/white; selected surface = warm gray; navigational text = near-white on charcoal; metadata = `#656a62` on white; destructive = `#af3d35`.
- Component: sidebar 240 px with a 12 px outer inset; workspace gutters 44 px; card radius 15 px; panel radius 18 px; controls 42–46 px; dialog radius 24 px.
- Font: bundled Manrope for Latin UI, Cormorant Garamond for editorial Latin numerals; Chinese body PingFang SC, headings Songti SC. Body UI 13–15 px; note content 15–16 px; page heading 36–42 px. Metadata 11–12 px.
- Spacing: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 44 / 56.
- Motion: 180–240 ms; subtle lift and focus; respect reduced motion.

## Interaction

- Library: clear media-type tabs, searchable tag picker, removable active filters, gallery/list view preference, visible batch mode, Cmd/Ctrl+K search.
- Cards: larger media with preserved image aspect within a consistent frame, restrained labels, readable title and author, predictable hover preview and drag handle.
- Detail: media left, readable content right; arrow-key gallery navigation; explicit original-image viewer and download; Radix focus trapping and Esc dismissal.
- Mobile: off-canvas navigation with dismiss overlay, stacked detail viewer, horizontally scrollable batch actions, no document-level horizontal overflow.
- Every image/drag interaction has a button or keyboard equivalent. No settings or account access behavior changes.
